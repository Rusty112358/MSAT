# MSAT
USAF Maintenance Scheduling Application Tool
<BR><BR>
System Purpose<BR>
The system identified within this Software Users Manual (SUM) is the Maintenance Scheduling Application Tool (MSAT), developed for the US Air Force. <BR> <BR>
MSAT provides an invaluable tool to analyze critical maintenance records.  It provides Aircraft Schedulers and Maintenance Managers with the information they need to make critical decisions in meeting the rigorous and challenging mission requirements facing Air Force leaders.  Previously, Aircraft Schedulers used a manual process to reconcile aircraft Special Inspection (SI), Time Change Items (TCI), Time Compliance Technical Order (TCTO) requirements, and Flying Hour source files.  This was accomplished by; establishing a standard by aircraft serial number then comparing the standard against the Core Automated Maintenance System (CAMS) Aircraft Planning Requirements (PRA) product.  This product, along with individual aircraft files contained thousands of records, which were manually cross-referenced with hundreds of job standards from the Job Master List (JML).  This manual line-by-line reconciliation, required by Air Force Instruction 21-101, was time consuming and expensive. <BR><BR>
MSAT automates these critical processes by utilizing Monarch ™ Data Mining software to convert existing CAMS, Reliability and Maintainability Information System (REMIS), Comprehensive Engine Management System (CEMS) and Supply text files so they can be imported into a Microsoft (MS) Access® database.  Queries are then used to produce various reports, which re-focus job performance on the utilization of this information, not on producing it.  Utilizing this process of producing reports reduces the risk of manual errors, and standardizes the way this information is utilized throughout the Air Force.  MSAT also eliminates the need for thousands of printed data pages, which were required to research information, as it existed in CAMS, REMIS, CEMS and Supply.  MSAT has been designed to support all Wings and multiple Mission Design Series (MDS) – it is not aircraft specific. <BR><BR>
Decision quality information-- MSAT allows Aircraft Schedulers and Maintenance Managers to focus on utilizing information made available by today’s automated systems, not recreating it. <BR><BR>
The MSAT Software Users Manual and on-line help file(s) have been generated to provide an overview of MSAT software, to include the operational usage, installation, new features, and system maintenance. <BR><BR><BR>
2 – Overview and Summary <BR><BR>
Key Features<BR><BR>
MSAT is an Air Force standard system for querying aircraft scheduling information and reports from base level systems.  The software has been developed to support the Department of Defense (DoD), Electronic Commerce/Electronic Data Interchange (EC/EDI) Paperwork Reduction Act initiatives. <BR><BR>
The system enhances aircraft maintenance automation procedures and increases its efficiency by producing reports which current base level systems do not support.  MSAT enables the AF to increase efficiency by: <BR>
•	Allowing a large volume of critical information to become deployable<BR>
•	Quickly retrieving aircraft, engine, Aerospace Ground Equipment (AGE), and armament maintenance information from base level systems <BR>
•	Providing drop down List of Values (LOV) data for report field selection criteria <BR>
•	Producing reports <BR>
•	Reducing paper use <BR>
•	Providing traceability of Inspections, Time Changes and TCTO’s for aircraft, engine, AGE, and armament<BR>
•	Providing traceability of aircraft Flying Hours <BR>
•	Tracking Alternate Mission Equipment inspections when both on and off the aircraft <BR>
•	Tracking  MICAP and DIFM  parts for aircraft engines and AGE <BR>
•	Providing Contingency Impact Analysis (CIA)<BR>
•	Providing a multiple unit capability within a single database<BR>
•	Providing weapons managers at higher headquarters and wing levels enhanced flexibility; by allowing the consolidation and display of existing automated products within a single database.<BR><BR>
